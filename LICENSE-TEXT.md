# The text's licence

`data/euclid-heath.txt` is adapted from the Perseus Digital Library's TEI text of Euclid's
*Elements* in Thomas Heath's translation, file `data/tlg1799/tlg001/tlg1799.tlg001.perseus-eng2.xml`
at commit `70427323b61267cdcd0256b9b02009c23392de07` of https://github.com/PerseusDL/canonical-greekLit,
which Perseus releases under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The adapted text is released under the same licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution: the Perseus
Digital Library, Tufts University (Gregory Crane, editor in chief); The Thirteen Books of Euclid's
Elements, translated by Thomas L. Heath (Cambridge University Press, 1908). Changes: the TEI
markup, headings, figures, Heath's introductions, notes, daggers and citations, and the labels
Perseus added removed; two typos in the source corrected; normalized to ASCII. `clean.py` in this
repository makes every change, and its docstring and comments list them.

Euclid's *Elements* (c. 300 BC) and Heath's translation (1908; Heath died in 1940) are in the
public domain.
